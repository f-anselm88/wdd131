Language and heritage

Discover Rukwangali Language and Culture

Language is more than vocabulary. It is also a way to share experiences, preserve knowledge, build relationships, and connect generations.

A Language of Connection

Rukwangali is associated with communities in Namibia's Kavango region. Learning a language can help people communicate across generations and better understand the experiences of the people who use it.

Eraka Lyetu provides a starting point for exploring vocabulary, expressions, and language-learning resources.

Stories and Oral Traditions

Stories, songs, sayings, and conversations can pass knowledge from one generation to another. Learning about these forms of expression can help learners understand how language is used in real-life contexts.

Community members and fluent speakers are important sources of knowledge when documenting expressions, pronunciation, and cultural context.

Why Language Preservation Matters

Knowledge

Language helps communities communicate knowledge, experiences, and ways of understanding the world.

Identity

Language can strengthen connections between people, families, communities, and their heritage.

Learning

Accessible learning materials can help new learners explore a language and encourage further study.

Share What You Know

If you are a fluent speaker or have reliable information about Rukwangali vocabulary and cultural expressions, your knowledge can help improve this learning resource.
