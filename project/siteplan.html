Eraka Lyetu Site Plan

Website planning document by Anselm Munango

Site Name

Eraka Lyetu

The name is Rukwangali for "our language." It tells visitors right away that the site celebrates and teaches the Rukwangali language, and it gives learners from the Kavango regions a sense of shared ownership.

Optional domain availability: erakalyetu.org

Site Purpose

Eraka Lyetu is a learning hub that helps people read, speak, and understand Rukwangali. The site provides:

  • Beginner-friendly lessons organized by topic, such as greetings, family, and numbers.
  • A searchable vocabulary and phrase list with English translations and pronunciation notes.
  • Short cultural notes that explain how the language is used in everyday life.
  • A contact form for learners, teachers, and community members who want to contribute words or corrections.

Scenarios

These are questions a visitor from the target audience (learners and heritage speakers) might ask:

  1. How do I greet an elder politely in Rukwangali?
  2. Where can I find a list of basic words for a first-time learner?
  3. How can I suggest a missing word or report a mistake?

Typography

Two fonts from Google Fonts are used. Both are free, easy to read on small screens, and have a fallback in case they fail to load.

Merriweather Bold

Headings and the site name

Fallback: Georgia, serif

Source Sans 3

Body text, navigation, buttons, and captions. It is clean and stays readable at small sizes, which suits lesson content and vocabulary lists.

Fallback: Arial, sans-serif
