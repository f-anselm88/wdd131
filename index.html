About Me

My name is Anselm Munango. I was born and raised in the Kavango region of Namibia and now live in Windhoek. I'm a junior full-stack developer, currently pursuing a BS in Software Development through BYU-Idaho and BYU-Pathway Worldwide. I served a two-year mission in Ghana, run a small side business called The Catalist, and I'm building a language-learning platform for my native language, Rukwangali.

Windhoek, Namibia

Namibia is located in southwestern Africa, bordered by the Atlantic Ocean. It's home to the Namib, one of the world's oldest deserts, and to a rich mix of cultures and languages, including Rukwangali, spoken in the Kavango region where I grew up.
